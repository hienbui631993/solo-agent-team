# Solopreneur skills: the full map

*Researched 11 October 2026 (second, deeper version). Sources are at the bottom.*

## The short answer

A solopreneur needs **12 skills in 4 layers**. You don't need to be an expert in all of them. You need to be **good at layers 1 and 2**, and **good enough** at layers 3 and 4.

| Layer | What it answers | Skills |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Strategy | Who do I serve, with what? | Niche, offer, pricing |
| 2. Marketing | How do people find me and buy? | Copywriting, lead generation, content and short video, lead capture, selling by text |
| 3. Keeping customers | How do they stay and bring friends? | Retention and referrals |
| 4. The engine | How does it run without me doing everything? | Systems and AI, numbers, money basics |

**Why this matters:** 34% of solopreneurs say getting customers is their biggest problem, second only to time (41%). Over 60% say they underestimated how hard it is to do everything alone ([Founder Reports](https://founderreports.com/solopreneur-statistics/)).

## Layer 1: Strategy (decide before you market)

### Skill 1. Choosing a niche
A niche is a specific group of customers with a specific problem. "Dental clinics in one city that want more braces patients" is a niche. "Businesses that need marketing" is not.

Why it wins ([MYB Workshops](https://mybworkshops.com.au/why-solopreneurs-need-a-niche/)):
- You can **charge more**, because you solve one urgent problem well.
- Your **marketing is clearer and cheaper**.
- People **refer you more**, because it's easy to say "they do X for Y".

**How to choose:** list 20 ideas, remove the ones with legal problems or low profit, then score the rest: **demand 30%, profit 30%, your fit 20%, rules/compliance 10%, how different you can be 10%**. Test the top 3 cheaply. Starting narrow doesn't lock you in. You can grow into nearby groups later.

### Skill 2. Making an offer
A good offer is clear, solves one painful problem, and feels low-risk to say yes to.
- **Learn from:** *$100M Offers* by Alex Hormozi.
- **Practice:** write your offer in one sentence. Ask 5 people if they understand it in 5 seconds.

### Skill 3. Pricing
The most common mistake is **pricing too low** ([Simply Business](https://www.simplybusiness.com/resource/common-mistakes-new-solopreneurs-make-and-how-to-avoid-them/)). Low prices attract difficult clients and leave no money to grow.
- **Prefer monthly (recurring) prices** over one-time projects. One-time work causes "feast or famine": busy one month, empty the next ([Lifestarr](https://www.lifestarr.com/blog/the-real-reason-solopreneurs-cant-get-to-predictable-revenue-in-2026)).
- **Ask for a deposit** or payment up front.

## Layer 2: Marketing (get seen, get chosen)

### Skill 4. Copywriting
Every message, post, ad, and page is writing. The structure: **grab attention → hold it → give value → ask for action.**
- **Practice:** write 1 hook a day. Ask AI for 10 versions and pick the best.

### Skill 5. Lead generation: the "Core Four"
There are only 4 ways to get customers (Hormozi, *$100M Leads*, [Shortform summary](https://www.shortform.com/blog/?p=146297)):

| Method | What it is | Needs talking? | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm outreach | Message people who know you | By text | Free |
| Cold outreach | Message strangers | By text | Free |
| Free content | Posts and videos that attract people | No | Free, slow |
| Paid ads | Pay Facebook or TikTok to show you | No | Costs money, fast |

**Volume is the secret.** The "Rule of 100": 100 marketing actions a day for 100 days. Most beginners fail because they do too little.

### Skill 6. Content and short video
- **One idea → many posts.** Dan Koe made about **$4.2 million in 2023** as a one-person business. He spends about **1 hour every morning** on one weekly newsletter, then turns it into posts for every platform ([Beginner Creator](https://beginnercreator.beehiiv.com/p/dan-koe-built-42-million-oneperson-business-running-money-failed-businesses)). His rule: **give information free, sell the doing.**
- **Short video:** the first 3 seconds (the hook) decide if people keep watching ([Metricool hook examples](https://metricool.com/viral-video-hooks/)). You can make "faceless" videos with AI voice and screen text.

### Skill 7. Lead capture (turning viewers into contacts)
Most people who see you won't buy today. You need a way to **keep in touch**: an **email list** or **messaging-app followers**.
- Give something free in exchange for their contact. This is called a **lead magnet**.
- In a study of **41,000+ signup forms**, the average signup rate was **22%**. The best types were **giveaways (29%), learning resources (27%), and interactive tools like quizzes or calculators (26%)**. Discounts were the weakest (16%) ([MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com/blog/best-lead-magnet-statistics)).
- Example for clinics: a free "Facebook health check" of their page.

### Skill 8. Selling by text
Answer fast, handle "too expensive", and ask for the yes. Save your answers to the 5 most common objections as templates.

## Layer 3: Keeping customers

### Skill 9. Retention and referrals
Getting a new customer is the hardest part, so keeping them is the cheapest growth.
- **Monthly retainers** give predictable income. Turn your best one-time clients into monthly clients ([Lifestarr](https://www.lifestarr.com/blog/the-real-reason-solopreneurs-cant-get-to-predictable-revenue-in-2026)).
- **63% of solopreneurs** get growth mainly from word of mouth ([Founder Reports](https://founderreports.com/solopreneur-statistics/)). So ask every happy client for a referral, and make it easy.
- Send a short monthly report so clients **see** the results.

## Layer 4: The engine

### Skill 10. Systems and AI
The 5 parts: **one database, automations, AI agents, written playbooks, a dashboard.**
- **64% of solopreneurs** already use AI for marketing ([Founder Reports](https://founderreports.com/solopreneur-statistics/)), so AI alone isn't an advantage. How well you connect it is.
- **Tools:** Google Sheets or Airtable, n8n or Make, Claude, Meta Business Suite.
- **A business messaging account** (on the chat app your customers use most) lets you connect chatbots and automations to your conversations.

### Skill 11. Numbers
Track every week: **people reached, leads, sales, money in**, plus **how many leads you need for 1 sale** and **how many clients leave each month**. If one number is weak, you know exactly what to fix.

### Skill 12. Money basics
From [Simply Business](https://www.simplybusiness.com/resource/common-mistakes-new-solopreneurs-make-and-how-to-avoid-them/):
- Keep **business and personal money separate** (a separate bank account).
- **Set aside part of every payment for tax.** In the US the advice is 30–40%. Rules differ by country, so check with a local accountant.
- Have **3–6 months of living costs** saved before quitting a job.

## Why solopreneurs fail (and how to avoid it)

1. **No clear niche or offer.** They try to serve everyone. *Fix: Layer 1 first.*
2. **Too little marketing.** *Fix: daily volume (Rule of 100).*
3. **They stop marketing when busy**, then have no clients next month. *Fix: a small daily marketing habit that never stops* ([Simply Business](https://www.simplybusiness.com/resource/common-mistakes-new-solopreneurs-make-and-how-to-avoid-them/)).
4. **Pricing too low.** *Fix: price on value, monthly.*
5. **Doing everything by hand.** *Fix: automate repeated tasks.*
6. **Working alone.** No feedback, no accountability. *Fix: join a community or find a peer.*

## Your 30-day learning plan

Learn by doing. Each week ends with a real result.

| Week | Learn | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Niche, offer, pricing | Score 3 niche ideas. Write your offer. Set a monthly price. |
| 2 | Copywriting + lead generation | Read *$100M Leads* summary. Pick 1 method. Do 20+ actions a day. |
| 3 | Content, short video, lead capture | Make 1 main piece → 7 posts or videos. Create 1 free lead magnet. Set up a business messaging account. |
| 4 | Systems, numbers, retention | Build your database and 1 automation. Track your numbers. Ask for 1 referral. |

**Books to start with:** *$100M Offers* and *$100M Leads* by Alex Hormozi (free summaries online).

**The biggest lesson:** skill comes from volume. The people who win do the same simple actions many times, measure, and improve.
