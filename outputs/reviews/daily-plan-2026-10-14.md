## Pushback
The weekly-review skill says one task each day must be a direct marketing or sales action. During a private trial there is nothing to sell, so task 3 is the closest honest substitute: it starts the customer research that all selling depends on. From week 3 this rule applies again in full.

## Why I did it this way
- **Context before agents.** An agent is only as good as what it reads first. Ten minutes on `context/` saves rewriting every draft later.
- **Your confusion is product research.** The places where setup trips you up are exactly where a buyer will get stuck, so logging them now writes the kit's README for free.
- **Real words beat guesses.** The customer profile is still a guess. Ten real quotes will tell you if the problems we listed are the ones people actually feel.
