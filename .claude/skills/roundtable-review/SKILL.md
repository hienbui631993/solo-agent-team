# Roundtable Review

*Usually run by the **chief-of-staff** agent. Read `context/` and `sops/approval-rules.md` first.*

## Inputs
- The document or decision (e.g. a proposal, offer, campaign brief, landing page).
- All of `context/`.

## Steps
1. Ask each agent for a short review from its angle:
   - **researcher**: Does it match what customers actually say? Any claim without evidence?
   - **strategist**: Is the offer clear? Is the price right? What's the risk?
   - **content-creator**: Is the message clear and in the customer's words?
   - **sales-assistant**: What objections will people have? Is the next step easy?
   - **analyst**: Are the numbers right? Can we measure success?
   - **designer**: Is it easy to read and on brand (if it's visual)?
2. Each reviewer must give at least one real concern. No "looks great" reviews.
3. Combine into: top 3 problems, possible made-up facts to check, and a fixed version or a list of edits.

## Output
Save `outputs/reviews/roundtable-[name]-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## Quality check
- Disagreement is good. Flag any statement nobody can back up as `[CHECK]`.

## Teach the user
After the output, add a short section called **Why I did it this way**: 2–3 lessons in simple English that explain the skill behind the choices. End with one short question that checks the user understood.
