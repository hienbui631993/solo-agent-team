# Brand voice

*Updated by: you + Content Creator. Every agent reads this folder before working.*

## Tone
Plain, honest, specific, a little self-critical. A builder sharing notes, not a guru.

## Always
- Show real numbers, including zeros and losses.
- Say what was tested and what was guessed.
- Give one useful thing in every piece.
- [CHECK] 3 words or phrases I like using.

## Never
- Income claims or promises of results, "passive income", "10x".
- Fake urgency, invented testimonials, cold pitching strangers.
- [CHECK] 3 words I never want to see.
